# Scientific Branding & Product Identity

## Overview

A great product identity is neither cosmetic decoration nor generic AI output. It is **cognitive ergonomics**: the science of reducing friction in how the human brain processes, remembers, and trusts a product.

Every brand identity rests on a triadic neurobiological framework:
1. **The Sound (Phonetics):** The biomechanics of the vocal tract and phonosemantic emotional response.
2. **The Light (Wavelength):** Retinal photopic efficiency, ciliary muscle strain, and evolutionary ecological valence.
3. **The Shape (Geometry):** Gestalt pre-attentive visual processing (V1 cortex), Prägnanz, and semiotic character.

When all three pillars align, the brand feels natural, memorable, and deeply authentic. When they are decoupled, the product feels generic, corporate, or artificial.

---

## When to Use

* Naming a new software, SaaS platform, CLI tool, or independent product.
* Redesigning or rebranding an existing product with a dated, bureaucratic, or cliché name.
* Choosing the primary brand hue (matiz) for a product interface based on cognitive load and user emotional state.
* Designing or evaluating a vector logo mark (SVG / Favicon / Monogram).
* Escaping the "AI Slop" trap (concentric radar waves, generic gradient swooshes, robotic suffixes like `-Bot` or `-AI`).

## When NOT to Use

* Naming internal code variables, functions, or database columns.
* Writing transient ad copy, marketing slogans, or social media posts.
* Tweaking micro-layout spacing (padding, margins, alignment).

### Pillar 1: Acoustic Phonetics & Naming (The Sound)

Studies in cognitive linguistics (notably led by Dr. Bodo Winter at the University of Birmingham) prove that phonemes trigger automatic emotional and visual responses before lexical comprehension occurs.

#### 1. Phonetic Fluency & Vocal Tract Ease
* **Open & Continuous Vowels (A, O, E):** Allow unobstructed airflow through the oral cavity, inducing feelings of lightness, clarity, and calm.
* **Smooth Consonants (Liquids & Nasals: L, M, N, R, V):** Require minimal muscular effort from the articulators (tongue, lips), creating an acoustic "glide" that the brain interprets as sophisticated and friction-free.
* **Harsh Stops (Plosives: T, K, P, B):** Interrupt the airstream abruptly. While useful for high-impact sports brands, they create friction when repeated or combined in tech co-pilots.

#### 2. The Bouba / Kiki Acoustic Effect
* **Bouba (Round & Soft):** Continuous vowels + liquid/nasal consonants (*Malu, Owen, Sona, Leme*). Evoke warmth, comfort, psychological safety, and low anxiety.
* **Kiki (Sharp & Fast):** Plosive consonants + high vowels (*Kip, Tekt, Patrick*). Evoke speed, cutting precision, aggression, and mechanical rigidity.
* **Calibration:** A product that eases user anxiety (e.g., job hunting, personal finance) requires a **70% Bouba / 30% Kiki** balance: calming vocal flow with a crisp terminal anchor.

#### 3. Cognitive Fluency & The Exposure Paradox
* The brain conserves glucose by favoring information that is effortless to pronounce and spell.
* **The Mere Exposure Balance:** The name must reside within a culturally familiar phonetic landscape, but with an exclusive structural twist. Too common (*"VagasBot"*) is dismissed as generic commodity; too alien (*"Xyloq"*) triggers cognitive rejection.

#### 4. Archetypal Resonance
* Avoid functional descriptions (*"Estágio"*, *"Tracker"*, *"Bot"*).
* Leverage elevated archetypes:
  * **The Beacon / Radar:** Silent depth listening, horizon scanning (*Sona, Mira*).
  * **The Dawn / Origin:** Fresh start, career germination (*Alvor, Lume*).
  * **The Rudder / Navigation:** Calm, effortless steering (*Leme, Rumo*).

### Pillar 2: Visual Neurobiology & Primary Hue (The Light)

Color selection is a neurobiological decision, governed by wavelength physics and evolutionary survival mechanisms.

#### 1. Retinal Biomechanics & Photopic Peak Efficiency
* **CIE Photopic Sensitivity Curve (Human Eye Peak at 555 nm):**
  Human vision evolved under tree canopies and open savannas. The peak sensitivity of M and L retinal cones is centered exactly at **555 nm (Green spectrum)**.
  * **Processing Cost:** The green/sage spectrum requires the **lowest metabolic and caloric effort** from the ciliary eye muscles to achieve sharp focus.
  * **Fatigue Reduction:** Users spending hours screening data, listings, or code experience significantly less asthenopia (eye strain) in green/sage environments.
* **Wavelength Disparity:**
  * **Long waves (Red ~700 nm):** Focus behind the retina; force ciliary muscle contraction; trigger sympathetic nervous system arousal (cortisol, heart rate).
  * **Short waves (Blue ~450 nm):** Focus slightly in front of the retina; promote parasympathetic relaxation, but blue light scatters easily and can induce visual glare at night.

#### 2. Ecological Valence Theory (Palmer & Schloss, UC Berkeley, 2010)
Human color preference is the evolutionary sum of emotional experiences with natural objects of that hue:
* **Blue:** Universal preference (clear sky, clean drinkable water; almost nothing toxic in nature is blue).
* **Green / Sage:** Vitality, edible vegetation, healthy harvest, shelter, growth permission ("green light").
* **Amber / Yellow:** Sunlight, warmth, mental alertness, morning illumination.
* **Red:** Biological alarm (blood, wounds, fire, venomous aposematic markings). **Anti-pattern for stressful workflows.**

#### 3. Spatial Psychophysics (Advancing vs. Receding)
* **Advancing Hues (Kiki Colors - Red / Orange):** Exhibit chromostereopsis, appearing to leap forward off the screen. Create urgency and compress perceived time.
* **Receding Hues (Bouba Colors - Green / Deep Blue):** Create spatial depth, making interfaces feel airy, spacious, and relaxed.

### Pillar 3: Geometric Semiotics & Logo Mark (The Shape)

#### 1. Gestalt Law of Prägnanz & Pre-Attentive Vision
The primary visual cortex (**V1**) extracts silhouetted boundaries and mass balance within **13 to 50 milliseconds**, long before conscious cognitive decoding:
* The brain instinctively reduces complex visual stimuli to the simplest, most symmetric, and stable primitive form possible.
* A mark loaded with fine lines, 3D drop-shadows, and micro-gradients overtaxes V1 and fails to form a persistent synaptic memory.

#### 2. The Two Non-Negotiable Litmus Tests
1. **The 3-Second Napkin Test:** If a casual user cannot sketch the basic silhouette of the mark from memory on a paper napkin in 3 seconds, the mark has failed cognitive retention.
2. **The 16x16px Favicon Test:** The mark must function as a pure binary silhouette at 16x16 pixels inside a browser tab without losing legibility.

#### 3. Escaping the "AI Slop" Trap
Modern generative AI defaults to cliché tropes: concentric radar rings, generic interlocking swooshes, gradient circles, and literal icons. Authentic brand marks possess **character, human authorship, and conceptual tension**:

* **Territory A — The Sculpted Pebble / Wabi-Sabi:** Asymmetric, tactile, organic closed curves with variable stroke weight (inspired by nature, stones, or Japanese zen enso).
* **Territory B — The Scout / Metaphor with Soul:** A continuous single-line animal silhouette (e.g., a hound scouting the ground) that humanizes an autonomous engine.
* **Territory C — Analog Mechanical Hardware:** Industrial precision tools (compass needle, dial caliper, crosshair matrix) inspired by Braun and Teenage Engineering.
* **Territory D — The Architectural Monogram:** Dense Swiss typography with surgical geometric incisions (e.g., Bauhaus tape or the S-O monolithic fusion).

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

When a user asks to name, color, or brand a product, follow this strict 4-phase sequence:

### Phase 1: Naming & Phonetic Audit
1. **Deconstruct the Core Value:** What does the product actually do in secret? (e.g., *"Silently scans opportunities while the user sleeps"*).
2. **Discard Functional Baggage:** Eliminate all suffixes like `-Bot`, `-AI`, `-Finder`, `-Tracker`, and bureaucratic nouns.
3. **Generate 6-8 Candidates across Archetypal Families:**
   * *Family A: Navigation & Direction (Bouba/Kiki Balanced)*
   * *Family B: Origin & Germination (Warm & Human)*
   * *Family C: Silent Scout & Radar (Minimalist & Focused)*
4. **Score against the Phonetic Table:** Vowel continuity, liquid/nasal ratio, Bouba/Kiki profile, and pronunciation friction.

### Phase 2: Color Calibration & Design Tokens
1. **Assess the User's Emotional Baseline:**
   * Is the user stressed, anxious, or tired? $\rightarrow$ Target **Sage / Emerald (555 nm)** or **Warm Paper / Amber**.
   * Is the user doing cold analytical accounting? $\rightarrow$ Target **Balanced Slate / Navy**.
   * **Rule:** Never use pure saturated red or high-stress alert palettes as brand primaries in high-anxiety tools.
2. **Define the CSS Variable Palette (`:root`):**
   * `--brand-primary`: High-contrast accessible hue (WCAG AAA compliant).
   * `--brand-primary-light`: 10-15% tint for badge backgrounds.
   * `--canvas-bg`: Off-white or subtle paper tint to kill glare.
   * `--sidebar-bg`: Luminous glass gradient or solid obsidian with branded glow.

### Phase 3: Logo Mark Architecture
1. **Select an Authentic Conceptual Territory:** Refuse generic AI concentric circles. Propose 3-4 distinct directions (Organic Pebble, Scout Metaphor, Mechanical Hardware, Architectural Monogram).
2. **Draft in Pure Mathematical SVG:**
   * Use clean `viewBox="0 0 100 100"`.
   * Standardize stroke weights (8px to 11px) with `stroke-linecap="round"`.
   * Ensure mark scales down cleanly to a 16x16px squircle container.
3. **Build the HTML Preview:** Always present logo and typography options in an interactive, isolated HTML preview (e.g., `preview-*.html`) before modifying production files.

## Canonical Reference: The "Sona" Case Study

This case study documents the transformation of an autonomous tech internship hunter from an generic AI prototype into a distinctive, author-grade brand identity.

### 1. The Naming Transformation

| Parameter | Before: "EstágioBot" | After: "Sona" |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phonetic Anatomy** | Harsh dental plosive /t/ + plosive /b/ + hiatus /io/. | Liquid /n/ + open vowels /o/, /a/ + sibilant /s/. |
| **Vocal Effort** | High (abrupt oral block at terminal "Bot"). | Zero friction (glides across the vocal tract in 2 syllables). |
| **Bouba / Kiki** | Mismatched (dry, bureaucratic, rigid). | 65% Bouba (calming) / 35% Kiki (crisp terminal anchor). |
| **Cognitive Fluency** | Stigmatized (associated with spam bots & college red tape). | Clean, modern, international (evokes Sonar and resonance). |
| **Archetype** | Generic utility script. | The Silent Scout / Deep Water Navigation. |

### 2. The Color Transformation

* **Initial State:** Standard corporate electric cobalt blue (`#0D5BFF`). Cold, corporate, indistinguishable from LinkedIn or enterprise IT.
* **Calibrated State: Sona Sage (`#059669`)**:
  * **Photopic Alignment:** Centered at 555 nm peak retinal sensitivity. Calms the cortisol of anxious students applying for jobs.
  * **Design System Integration:** Paired with an electric luminous gradient sidebar (`#059669 → #046642`) and a crisp porcelain canvas (`#F4F7F6`), preserving modern SaaS energy with zero visual fatigue.

## Deliverable Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any brand identity:
1. **Phonetic Integrity:** Is the name completely devoid of plosive stutter and generic tech suffixes?
2. **Photopic Comfort:** Does the primary hue match the cognitive task and avoid artificial eye strain?
3. **Silhouetted Distinctiveness:** Does the mark pass the 3-Second Napkin Test and 16px Favicon Test?
4. **Anti-Slop Verification:** Does the mark avoid literal stock metaphors and generic AI clichés?
5. **Interactive Preview Protocol:** Were design and typographic options presented to the user via standardized `preview-*.html` files?
